## 5. Gaps, restraint and discrepancies

1. **No novelty claim is made.** No source file contains a precedent or novelty sweep for this paper, and `paper.md` makes no "first" claim about its findings. The README's standard sources paragraph is retained in the house form; the paper itself claims no priority.
2. **Contested points are carried as flagged, not resolved:** the Takht-i-Bahi era identification (Azes = Vikrama), Senior's alternative Gondophares chronology, the Luoyang pillar dating, and the *Yishen Lun* internal dating to 642 all appear in the paper exactly as the source flags them (THOM method note).
3. **Absences are stated at the source's strength only.** The Jingjiao and Manichaean absences are "verified absences" in the surviving/translated corpus and identified catalogues, with the source's own caveat (§2 above); the Bulayïq absence is a survival statement in the source, and the paper says so.
4. **References** in the paper's method note reproduce THOM's principal-sources list only; no bibliographic detail (publisher, page range, DOI) has been added where the source does not give it.
5. **Data/code availability beyond the files** (repository, DOI, licence for the source build) is not stated in any source file; the paper claims none.
6. **Brief-vs-file discrepancies found: none material.** Every headline fact in the commissioning brief — the composition interval and its papyri/Hippolytus ceiling, the China NO BITE verdict and its carrier dates (635/638/745/781), the Northwest PLAUSIBLE–UNPROVEN grade with Gudnaphar and Gad, the Gondophares dates and era flag, the 1854 literary-witness point, the Origen allotment counter-evidence, the 16th-century first writing of the Malabar form, the c. 550 / c. 590 / c. 1298 South dates, and the FIRM anchors (c. 550; Kollam 849/850) — matches THOM as briefed.
